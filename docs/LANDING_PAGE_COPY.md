## Three ways to make Windows feel yours

**Follow your flow.** Give your pointer a glow, add click ripples, smooth your wheel or bring small details closer with a live magnifier.

**Clear your space.** Snap windows, dim distractions, keep system information close and find the text you just copied.

**Keep useful tools within reach.** Measure pixels, pick colors, show shortcuts and open everyday device controls from a floating dock.

## One place, your choices

One installer. A consistent settings hub. Shared shortcuts and profiles. Explicit capability grants. All features begin disabled, and you can stop everything with Ctrl+Alt+P.

## Private by default

No Nexus account. No telemetry service. Clipboard history stays in memory and disappears when the feature stops. Optional weather and publisher-configured update checks use the network only when requested.

## Built to keep

Native C#/.NET and WPF. Official Nexus releases are available for personal desktop use under the proprietary Nexus license. Your app continues working independently of an AI subscription.
